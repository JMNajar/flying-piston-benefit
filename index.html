The Mission

We Ride So
Kids Can Too.

Riders built this culture. We owe it to the next generation to keep the wheels turning. Every bid at the Flying Piston Benefit goes directly to All Kids Bike — a national program that puts Learn-to-Ride PE curricula into public school kindergartens across America.

Full kits. Helmets. Teacher training. The whole shot. Because the first ride changes everything — and every kid deserves that moment.

We're not just giving kids bikes — we're giving them freedom. That first ride? It can change everything.
— Wendy Koro, Title Sponsor · Russ Brown Motorcycle Attorneys
